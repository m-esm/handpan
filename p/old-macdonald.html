About this arrangement

Old MacDonald. Traditional, published 1917 in this form. Public domain. A public-domain melody ported from the Tonalics letter notes at tonalics.com/songs/old-macdonald, as written so that every note sits on this D Kurd pan. Range major sixth, pentatonic. Count ONE and two and three and four and, at 116 BPM. The melody is the only voice, on 3, 4, 5, 7, 8; the accent marks each bar's first beat. Add the ding under a downbeat when you want a bass, and let long notes ring through their rests.

Notation

Each line is one bar in 4/4. Numbers are the pan's fields, a dot is a rest, brackets hold notes struck together, and × is a tak on the shoulder. Read the number legend below.
