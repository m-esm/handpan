About this pattern

Count 1 2 3 4 5 6. The bass lands on 1 and a two-note chord on 4, and the tune sits in the gaps. Cradle is that rocking alone over the ding, low A, B-flat, C. Song puts a slow tune on top: high A down to F, then E to D, with long notes. Higher moves the same tune one field up the pan and peaks on the high C. Play it soft and let every chord ring.
