About this arrangement

London Bridge. Traditional English nursery rhyme, in print since 1744; the tune sung today was written down in the 19th century. Public domain. A public-domain melody ported from the Tonalics letter notes at tonalics.com/songs/london-bridge, as written so that every note sits on this D Kurd pan. Range major sixth, major, no accidentals. Count ONE and two and three and four and, at 109 BPM. The melody is the only voice, on 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8; the accent marks each bar's first beat. Add the ding under a downbeat when you want a bass, and let long notes ring through their rests.

Notation
