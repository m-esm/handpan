About this arrangement

Twinkle Twinkle Little Star. Traditional — melody is "Ah! vous dirai-je, maman", published 1761. Public domain. A public-domain melody ported from the Tonalics letter notes at tonalics.com/songs/twinkle-twinkle-little-star, as written so that every note sits on this D Kurd pan. Range major sixth, major, no accidentals. Count ONE and two and three and four and, at 99 BPM. The melody is the only voice, on 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8; the accent marks each bar's first beat. Add the ding under a downbeat when you want a bass, and let long notes ring through their rests.

Notation

Each line is one bar in 4/4. Numbers are the pan's fields, a dot is a rest, brackets hold notes struck together, and × is a tak on the shoulder. Read the number legend below.
