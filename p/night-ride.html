About this pattern

Every sixteenth is filled, so the hands trade without a gap. In Ride the low A is the quiet pulse and the ding is the loud 1. In Lift the tune sits on the beats and the pulse stays soft between them: loud, soft, soft, soft. Break stops the motor for two bars, then a run of four notes starts it again. Start at 70 and raise the tempo when the soft notes stay soft.
