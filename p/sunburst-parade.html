About this pattern

Let this one smile. The tune leans into F major, the bright relative of D Kurd, then returns to the ding. Count 1 and 2 and 3 and 4 and; keep the tempo steady through every rest. Spark introduces the hook, 6, 8, 9, 8, with space around it. Stride bounces between bass and melody. Sunburst opens the tune wide, with accents on 1, the and of 2, and 4. Shade is a quiet breath, not a slower tempo. Skylight climbs in answering phrases; Fireworks brings back the hook with paired notes and a tumbling reply. Keep the unaccented notes light so the melody dances above them. Learn Stride at 84 BPM, then Sunburst, and join the sections at 116. Let the last 0+1 ring.
