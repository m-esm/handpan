About this arrangement

Up on the Housetop. Benjamin Hanby, 1864. Public domain. The melody was not written down from memory. It is derived from a published transcription and compared, note for note, with independent ones: Followed: Douglas D. Anderson, The Hymns and Carols of Christmas (sequence released to the public domain) Checked against: Thornton Rose, An Online Christmas Songbook (2001) — 2 of 66 notes differ, as variants of a t. A public-domain melody ported from the Tonalics letter notes at tonalics.com/songs/up-on-the-housetop, as written so that every note sits on this D Kurd pan. Range major sixth, major, no accidentals. Count ONE and two and three and four and, at 128 BPM. The melody is the only voice, on 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8; the accent marks each bar's first beat. Add the ding under a downbeat when you want a bass, and let long notes ring through their rests.
