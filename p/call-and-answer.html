About this pattern

Talk is one bar low, then one bar high. The call moves around the low A, B-flat, and C. The answer copies its rhythm on the high A, high C, and G. In Closer the two halves share one bar, so the answer comes sooner. Leave the first three sixteenths of each answer empty. That gap is the timing of the piece.

Notation

Each line is one bar in 4/4. Numbers are the pan's fields, a dot is a rest, brackets hold notes struck together, and × is a tak on the shoulder. Read the number legend below.
