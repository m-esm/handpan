About this arrangement

Jolly Old Saint Nicholas. American, 19th century; the music was first published in 1874. Public domain. The melody was not written down from memory. It is derived from a published transcription and compared, note for note, with independent ones: Followed: Douglas D. Anderson, The Hymns and Carols of Christmas (sequence released to the public domain) Checked against: Thornton Rose, An Online Christmas Songbook (2001) —<. A public-domain melody ported from the Tonalics letter notes at tonalics.com/songs/jolly-old-saint-nicholas, moved down a fifth so that every note sits on this D Kurd pan. Range major sixth, major, no accidentals. Count ONE and two and three and four and, at 101 BPM. The melody is the only voice, on 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8; the accent marks each bar's first beat. Add the ding under a downbeat when you want a bass, and let long notes ring through their rests.
