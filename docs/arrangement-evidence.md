# Arrangement evidence and limits

Reviewed 2026-10-31. These are independently authored handpan performance forms. No course tabs, lyrics, source-score files or piano accompaniments are bundled. The active catalog, notation, durations and dates are derived in [the generated library](../p/index.html); `library: true` in `js/pieces.js` selects entries. English and Persian descriptions disclose the musical changes.

Technical playability does not establish melody accuracy. The six cover arrangements remain provisional. None has received a complete note-for-note comparison or a listening review by a musician. The original composition has no external melody to verify. A complete standalone form means an introduction, developed sections and an ending; it does not mean every layer or every bar of the commercial recording is reproduced.

## Sources actually consulted

- [Paul Simon's official song page](https://www.paulsimon.com/track/the-sound-of-silence/) identifies The Sound of Silence and its five-stanza form. It does not provide pitches. The handpan melody was reconstructed from musical recollection; its complete fidelity is unverified. [Hal Leonard's edition listing](https://www.halleonard.com/product/14031012/the-sound-of-silence) is a possible future score reference, not evidence of a completed score comparison.
- [The Beatles' official album page](https://www.thebeatles.com/let-it-be-0) establishes the recording context. [Sound Reason Studio's public working lead sheet](https://soundreasonstudio.com/MuseScore-Library-msv3-041119/Repertoire/Let%20it%20Be%20-%20Lead%20Sheet%201.pdf) was visually inspected for Let It Be's C-major opening contour and verse/refrain shape. The handpan accompaniment, instrumental break and voicings are new. This working sheet is not an authoritative Beatles transcription. The chorus peak is compressed and rhythmic details simplified; the whole tune still needs verification.
- [Hal Leonard's Feeling Good listing](https://www.halleonard.com/product/354090/feeling-good) identifies the Nina Simone edition. [Musicnotes' edition information](https://www.musicnotes.com/sheetmusic/nina-simone/feeling-good/MN0128948_U4) states the originally published G-minor key and free opening tempo. Hal Leonard's Closer Look preview returned unavailable. Neither source verifies the drafted D-minor melody. The metered calls, triplet accompaniment and original bridge need review against the recording.
- [Ramin Djawadi's Song Exploder interview](https://songexploder.net/transcripts/game-of-thrones-transcript.pdf) confirms the Game of Thrones main title's minor/major opening contrast, cello theme, later B section and sparse ending. [Jan Kolacek's public first-page preview](https://easypiano.cz/cs/system/files/pdf-1p/GameOfThronesMainTitle_1p.pdf) was visually inspected for its C-minor ostinato, 3/4 meter and opening melody. The handpan's D-minor version omits the F-sharp major turn, folds registers and supplies its own connecting development; it does not reproduce the film's entire B section or orchestration.
- [Mohsen Karbassi's Soltane Ghalbha page](https://www.mohsenkarbassi.com/en-us/iranian-piano-sheets/soltane-ghalbha/) identifies Rohani and Aref. Both pages of the [public F-minor score](https://www.mohsenkarbassi.com/media/iznljmfn/soltane_ghalbha_faminor.pdf) were visually inspected for 3/4 meter, upper rocking theme and contrasting lower theme. The piano accompaniment was not ported. The new two-hand accompaniment is independent. Transposition down a minor third, C5 for absent B-flat4, C natural for C-sharp, lower-register octave folds, omitted ornaments and slower tempo materially change the result. Recognizability after those changes remains unverified against Aref's recording.
- [WaterTower Music's official Day One audio listing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vPA6T0la6uI) identifies the selected Interstellar cue as Day One (Interstellar Theme), by Hans Zimmer. [WaterTower's soundtrack page](https://www.watertower-music.com/release/interstellar-original-motion-picture-soundtrack-expanded-edition/) supplies album context. The public publisher-score preview could not be inspected: the retailer returned HTTP 403 and the Score Exchange preview image was unavailable through web retrieval. The authored A-minor melody is therefore unverified and may need replacement after comparison with the recording. Identity evidence must not be treated as pitch evidence.

## Resource provenance

New cover files reuse existing repository illustrations: slow-rain for The Sound of Silence, first-light for Let It Be, alamut-at-dusk for Lanterns Across the Water, bright-morning for Feeling Good, storm-front for Game of Thrones, and night-ride for Day One. Soltane Ghalbha retains its existing cover. Share cards are generated from those covers and the actual catalog metadata. Historical unused illustrations are retained; their presence does not restore a library entry or an SEO page.

Dates are generated by `scripts/added.mjs`. For a never-committed ID, the first generated timestamp is provisional and reused on later runs so `--check` is stable. Once the ID is committed, its introduction commit becomes authoritative. Run the generator again after the parent commits the pieces.

Before claiming musical completion, a musician needs to compare each cover's full sounded melody with the identified recording or an authoritative score, then accept or revise the disclosed range substitutions. Browser playback proves routing and scheduling, not that musical review.
