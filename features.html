Features

What the practice app does today.

Current

  • LibraryEvery pattern is listed, with search and kind filters. Opening a row does not play audio.
  • PlayThe selected piece has the full score and transport. The handpan and score highlight the current and upcoming notes.
  • LearnOne bar at a time. Hear the phrase, try the notes, or repeat it slowly. Start learning picks a simple Hands drill. Continue learning restores the last phrase and tempo.
  • MicrophoneIn Play, the page shows the note you struck and waits until you strike the lit note.
  • ToolsMicrophone, Mirror, the landscape picker, and Explore. Phones group them under Tools.
  • LandscapesSix Persian mountain and courtyard settings. Explore lets you look around a place and choose a light to find music.
  • Phone motionEnable motion lets you tilt and rotate the 3D handpan. Recenter resets its neutral position.
  • Simple viewIf 3D takes too long, continue with the library and sheet.
  • KeyboardKeys 0–9 play the notes. 0 is the ding. T plays tak.
  • Instrument36 patterns for a Gear4music DISC 10-note D Kurd handpan. The ding is D3.

Limits

These are limits of the app today, not a roadmap.

  • MicrophoneMicrophone works in Play. Learn disables it and explains why.
  • ProgressLearn does not mark a piece learned and does not score timing.
  • Screen tapsScreen taps teach note order, not physical handpan accuracy.
  • LandscapesThe landscapes are artistic interpretations, not historical reconstructions.
