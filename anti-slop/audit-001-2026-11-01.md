## HIGH

1. **Explore Rise and Sink buttons work by pointer only.** `js/explore.js:260-277` binds `pointerdown`/`pointerup` and nothing else, and `js/explore.js:42-44` swallows Space while a button has focus. Enter or Space on Rise does nothing. The move and look pads (`js/explore.js:86-92`, `:105-111`) carry `role="slider"` with no `tabindex`, no key handler, and `aria-valuenow` frozen at 0. R-32, R-39. Fix: make Rise and Sink hold on `keydown`/`keyup` of Enter and Space, and either drop the slider role from the pads or make them focusable and arrow-key driven.

2. **Microphone errors tell the user to press a button that does not exist.** `js/i18n.js` keys `mic.blocked`, `mic.missing`, `mic.failed` say "press Listen"; the button is labelled "Microphone" (`index.html:61`, key `mic`). `mic.disconnected` already says Microphone. R-27 (an error state names the next action). Fix: say "press Microphone" in all four, English and Persian.

3. **Three error states are hard-coded English and skip i18n.** `js/motion.js:112` and `:114,:117` ("Motion access was not allowed", "Could not enable motion"), `js/main.js:2176` ("A microphone needs https or localhost", jargon with no next action), `js/print.js:72` (hand letters L/R in the printed book). Persian users see English. R-27, R-34 (every shipped locale must work). Fix: route all four through `t()` with Persian strings; reword the https line as "The microphone only works on a secure (https) page."

4. **Reduced motion still moves the sky.** `js/scene.js:26` reads `prefers-reduced-motion` once, with no change listener. Under reduce, aurora and rune rotation (`:1187`, `:1190`), particle time (`:1176`, `:1855`) and sky time (`:1846`) still use raw `seconds`, and `pendingFrames` bursts on each strike, focus change and scroll (`:1164`, `:1937-1946`, `:1981`) redraw them, so they jump after every note. R-39. Fix: feed those uniforms `still ? REST_SECONDS : seconds` like `writeCycle` does at `:1724`.

5. **Score slots are 32 px wide on phones with no gap.** Measured at 320 px: every `.slot` button is 32 x 52 (`css/app.css` `.bars` min column `calc(var(--cols) * 34px + 44px)`). Adjacent targets touch. R-03. Fix: raise the per-slot minimum to 40 px so a 7-slot bar takes a full row on phones, and give `.slot` an inset hit area.

6. **The notation legend is hover-only.** `js/main.js:1655` puts the whole legend, including "tap a bar number to loop it", in a `title` attribute on the sheet. Touch and keyboard users never see it in Play. `js/main.js:1442` and `:2204` do the same for the Share and Microphone hints. R-03 (hover-only), R-39. Fix: render the legend as a one-line `<p class="key">` under the sheet in Play (Learn already has "How to read and play").

7. **`llms.txt` overclaims.** `scripts/seo.mjs:335` writes "the pages say which notes were substituted", but only 4 of 10 piece pages carry substitution wording (game-of-thrones, in-the-end, soltan-e-ghalbha, lanterns). R-36. Fix: "where a song was transposed, its page says which notes were substituted", or add the sentence to the other six blurbs.
